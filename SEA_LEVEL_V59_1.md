# Sea-level treatment — V59.1

V59.1 retains only the **archived-FaIR-horizon persistence result** as paper-facing.

Central additional global mean sea level relative to 2026:
- 2100: 0.309 m
- 2300: 0.659 m
- 2400: 0.705 m
- 2400 rate: 0.281 mm/yr

That is the useful finding: the simple emulator still has sea level rising at the end of the archived FaIR horizon despite major surface cooling.

## AR6 comparison

IPCC AR6 assesses:
- SSP1-1.9 likely 2100 GMSL rise: 0.28–0.55 m relative to 1995–2014;
- SSP1-2.6 assessed 2300 range: 0.3–3.1 m relative to 1995–2014.

Sources:
https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-9/
