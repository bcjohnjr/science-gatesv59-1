# Figure captions — V59.1

## `v59_1_cumulative_drawdown_response_envelope.png`

Atmospheric CO₂ drawdown versus cumulative anthropogenic net removal after the first net-negative year. The three lines use the archived FaIR p05, p50 and p95 **concentration percentile curves**. They are not the p05/p50/p95 quantiles of the response fraction across the 841 configurations because the archived validation package does not contain per-configuration trajectories.

## `v59_1_apparent_response_fraction_envelope.png`

Apparent atmospheric response is the atmospheric CO₂ stock decrease divided by cumulative anthropogenic net removal after the first net-negative year. Values above one early in the pathway are not super-efficient removal: land and ocean remain natural net sinks for a time because of the preceding high-CO₂ state, so natural uptake contributes to the numerator while the denominator contains only anthropogenic net removal. As the natural sinks weaken and later reverse, the ratio falls. This is a path-dependent diagnostic, not a universal marginal removal efficiency.

The p05/p50/p95 lines are aggregate concentration-percentile-curve diagnostics only. A proper configuration-level uncertainty distribution requires a FaIR rerun that archives all individual configuration trajectories.
