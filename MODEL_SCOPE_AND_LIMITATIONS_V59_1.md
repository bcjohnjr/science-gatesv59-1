# Model scope and limitations — V59.1

V59.1 is a scope-freeze maintenance release.

The package preserves the integrated V59.0 architecture for lineage and supplementary analysis, but the intended manuscript claim is narrowed to externally benchmarked carbon-cycle response and its financing consequence.

Main unresolved limitations:
- no configuration-level FaIR response-fraction distribution in the archived aggregate output;
- inverse external-model solve not yet run;
- OSCAR gate not yet closed;
- sea-level component emulator is not a process-based ice-sheet model and under-disperses AR6 2300 uncertainty;
- pH screen fixes temperature/salinity and is not a substitute for CO2SYS/PyCO2SYS;
- regional coral, local sea level, fisheries and social outcomes remain separate screens rather than the paper's physical core.
